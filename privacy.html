Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Steal an Egg Videos handles information when using its video preparation and TikTok draft-upload functionality.

Information We Access

When a user authorizes the service through TikTok's official OAuth authorization process, the service may access limited TikTok account information permitted by the user.

This may include basic profile information such as an account identifier, display name, and profile image, when made available through the user.info.basic permission.

Authorization Tokens

TikTok access and refresh tokens may be stored locally on the computer running the service so authorized TikTok API requests can be made.

TikTok passwords are not requested, collected, or stored by this service.

Video Information

The service may store local information about videos prepared for upload, including file names, processing status, TikTok upload status, and TikTok publish or upload identifiers used to track draft-upload progress.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to operate the video preparation, account authorization, draft-upload, status-checking, and recovery features of the service.

TikTok Draft Uploads

Videos may be sent to TikTok using TikTok's official Content Posting API as drafts for further review and editing by the authorized user.

The draft-upload workflow does not automatically publish a public TikTok post.

Data Storage

Application data, authorization tokens, and video-processing records are stored locally by the operator of the service unless otherwise required by TikTok's systems to provide the requested functionality.

Data Sharing

The service does not sell personal information.

Information is shared with TikTok only as necessary to use TikTok's official authentication and Content Posting APIs.

Data Retention

Local authorization and processing information may be retained for as long as necessary to operate the service, prevent duplicate uploads, recover interrupted uploads, or maintain upload history.

Revoking Access

Users may revoke TikTok authorization through TikTok's account or connected-app settings where available. Local authorization data can also be removed from the device running the service.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to avoid exposing access tokens, refresh tokens, client secrets, and other authentication credentials. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

This service is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children through its own website.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the service changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the project's public GitHub repository.
