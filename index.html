Steal an Egg Videos

A video preparation and draft-upload utility for creating short-form video content and sending approved videos to TikTok for further review, editing, and manual publishing.

About

Steal an Egg Videos helps prepare short-form video clips for social media. Videos are reviewed locally before being sent to TikTok through TikTok's authorized developer services.

Uploaded videos are sent as drafts for additional review and editing. The service does not automatically publish public posts during the draft-upload workflow.

Account Access

TikTok account access is authorized through TikTok's official OAuth authorization system. The service does not request or store a user's TikTok password.
